Trace analyzes suspicious communications, retrieves cybersecurity guidance, and checks whether its conclusions are supported by evidence.

How Trace works

01 · Evidence retrieval

Hybrid BM25 and semantic search retrieve relevant cybersecurity documentation.

02 · Evidence reranking

A cross-encoder ranks retrieved passages for relevance to the suspicious message.

03 · Claim verification

When enabled, Trace checks assessment claims against the retrieved evidence.

04 · Final assessment

Trace reports risk indicators, supporting evidence, uncertainty, and safe actions.
